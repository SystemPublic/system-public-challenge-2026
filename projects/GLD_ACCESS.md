# GLD integration status
The GLD manual and access documentation were reviewed; no GLD observations were used. Survey access is source-specific.

Before integration record country, survey name, survey year, access route/license, harmonization version, unit of observation, employment/working-age definitions, hours concept, formality definition, sampling weight and variance design. Select a country-survey-year only after checking availability and authorization, then compare derived totals with its published survey tables.

Access documentation: https://worldbank.github.io/gld/Support/A%20-%20Guides%20and%20Documentation/GLD%20Manual%20Files/GLD%20content,%20storage,%20and%20access.html
