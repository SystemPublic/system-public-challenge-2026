Use 3 · The graph says in advance which tests cannot succeed

Multiplying effect sizes along a chain predicts the city effect before any data are read

Cold days → asthma → school absence

1.007

Predicted ratio of absence on cold days (1.001 to 1.017). A claim was registered knowing the test could not separate this from no effect, and it is scored unsettled.

Re-timed work week → health

~0.1%

Predicted change. The city health survey detects only differences of a few percent.

The check tells a team in advance what a test can decide. School absence is 10% higher on days at or below 0 °C (5 to 15). The asthma chain predicts 0.7%, which is about 8% of the observed rise on the log scale.

The claim is graph-triad-cold-asthma-absence, a bridge. The endpoint is 3.8 standard errors above the composed prediction and D = 3.0, below the 4 that rule 8 requires. No test was registered for the re-timed week. Rule 8 in the project: an endpoint test must discriminate between the chain and no effect before it can decide anything.
