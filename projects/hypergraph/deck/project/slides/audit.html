Use 6 · The city audits the graph

Read against abstracts: 14 records wrong and 12 without a qualifier of 62 on heat and medicines; 6 and 19 of 114 on city interventions

  • “81% reduced odds” stored as an odds ratio of 0.81
  • Percentages stored as ratios; reference groups named as exposures
  • Findings about a satellite instrument named for ozone filed under ozone, which produced the largest city–graph disagreement in the matrix
  • Eviction moratoriums grounded to Eupatorium, a plant genus
  • In 12 of the 15 most evenly split urban relationships the split is not real: “cognitive impairment” and “cognitive function” share one concept

A city’s data are an independent check on extracted research. Each disagreement points to records to repair.

System's own documentation estimates about 20% of associations are mis-extracted. Our reading of 114 records in the intervention matrix found 6 wrong and 19 that had lost a qualifier, 22% together. For heat and medicines the two together are 26 of 62, or 42%. 27% of relationships that report findings returned none through the API in one experiment.
