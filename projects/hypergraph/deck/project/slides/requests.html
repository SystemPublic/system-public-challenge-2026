Three requests to System

Small changes to the graph would make city work much easier

1. Store direction

Record which variable of a finding is the exposure, and whether a variable name is negated. Most candidate chains failed because the middle concept was a consequence of both ends.

2. Make findings retrievable

Return every finding counted in a relationship through the API, and report the signs in the relationship record. Half of well-studied relationships mix signs.

3. Ground urban exposures

Commuting, transit, cycling, eviction, vacant land and congestion pricing exist only as variable names. Urban topics are about 2% of the graph.

These come from the audit work. They are offered as the feedback a public beta is for.
