A strip with rival conjectures

Five reasons heat might raise shootings, and a test that narrows them.

Registered this morning before the counts were read. Outside 1.17 per 5 °C, inside 1.01. The literature supplied the conjectures, and the city's record narrowed them.

The edge from heat to shootings is solid in two cities, and the reason is open. The literature offers at least five reasons. Two act through behaviour: heat brings people outdoors where they meet, and heat raises drinking (Parks 2023, alcohol visits up 25%). Three act on the body: short temper (a laboratory meta-analysis finds no reliable effect), sleep loss after hot nights, and medicines that impair cooling (no study of medication, heat and violence exists). The conjectures disagree about shootings indoors: a bodily effect acts wherever the person is, and contact outdoors does not. The police record inside or outside for every shooting. Two claims were registered on 14 November before the dated counts were fetched. From 2006 to 2026, outside shootings rise 1.174 (1.155 to 1.192) per 5 °C on 20,958 incidents and inside shootings 1.009 (0.975 to 1.044) on 3,471. The ratio of the two slopes is 0.861 (0.828 to 0.896, the referee's corrected interval). A bodily effect that follows people indoors is not seen. The test does not separate more contact outdoors from a direct effect of heat felt outdoors, since most indoor spaces are cooled; sleep and medication were not tested with lags. Inside shootings are a different mix of events, and violence in prisons without cooling does rise with heat. Details: docs/conjectures.md.
