The question

What can the graph and a city’s open data do together that neither does alone?

Confirm

A relationship seen both in the research and in the city’s data

Contest

Research and city disagree, with a statement of what would settle it

Bridge

A chain no single dataset holds: trees to temperature to emergency visits

Most results fall between these. “Unsettled” was the most common verdict, and the method treats it as a result.

The challenge text asks for confirmations, conflicts with what would settle them, and bridges, plus a dashboard and a map of research needed. Our addition is the fourth category, unsettled, which is where most honest tests land.
