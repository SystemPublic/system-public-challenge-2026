Edges, not variables: what research and a city’s data know about each link

The System graph links variables by findings from papers. We treat each link as a running meta-analysis and add New York’s registered test to it.

94 predictions registered and 93 scored on versioned city data. A referee who had not seen the reasoning re-estimated 87 of the 93 outcomes. Twelve claims are packaged for replication.
