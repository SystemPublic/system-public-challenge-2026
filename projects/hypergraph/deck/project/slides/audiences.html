Two organisations, two interests

The graph and the challenge come from different places

System, which built the graph

A venture-funded company founded by Adam Bly. Its unit is a statistical association between two variables, extracted by a language model from a published study and linked to a concept. The paid product is clinical knowledge for other products. The Public Graph is a free, non-commercial snapshot, in beta, released with Wellcome support.

What it needs from us: evidence that the graph is useful outside medicine, and the errors it makes there.

Knowledge Futures, which set Challenge 4

A nonprofit spun out of MIT, led by Travis Rich, which makes PubPub and the Underlay. The Underlay stores versioned assertions with their provenance.

What it needs from us: relationships sorted into confirmed, contested and bridged, each citing the dataset versions it rests on, with the history of how each verdict was reached.

Be explicit that these are two audiences. System wants to know what the graph helps with and where it fails outside its clinical home. Knowledge Futures wants to see that a versioned claim graph with provenance helps people reason about evidence. The talk serves both: the findings and the audit for System, the registration and referee history for Knowledge Futures.
