Use 2 · The graph shows literatures that do not cite each other

A day moved from office to home: two bodies of research predict opposite effects

Commuting studies

Less commuting gives better sleep, better mood, less back pain. Includes designs that follow the same people over time.

Telework studies

More work at home gives worse sleep, worse mood, more back pain. Thirteen of sixteen abstracts read are surveys from 2020 and 2021.

They disagree in sign on 6 of 10 shared outcomes. A search for “hybrid work and health” finds only the second. City data show a larger gap: Monday and Friday subway entries in the business district are 13.8% below midweek, up from 5.1% in 2019. Whether hybrid work causes it was not tested.

This is the clearest case of the graph doing something a literature search does not: it puts two vocabularies for the same intervention side by side.
