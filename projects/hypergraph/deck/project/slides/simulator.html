0.29

The 2026 speed response as a share of the one fitted on 2025 (0.19 to 0.38). Two storm days carry 84% of the evidence, so the miss cannot be assigned to the link.

+$118M

Yearly revenue of a $12 toll on every weekday against today’s schedule (+90 to +150). Only revenue and entries have intervals that exclude zero.

−0.9%

Ambulance peak travel time in the zone relative to the rest of the city (−3.0 to +1.4). The test could not tell −1.8% from no effect.

Storefront vacancy in the toll zone did not rise by 1.2 points relative to the rest of the city. The graph supplied one link: each minute less of response time raises the odds of surviving cardiac arrest by 1.02 to 1.24.

Say “did not rise by 1.2 points relative to the rest of the city”, not “vacancy did not rise”. Day-level data cannot identify the speed relation in either year.
