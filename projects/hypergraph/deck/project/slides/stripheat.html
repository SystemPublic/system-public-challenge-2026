A strip: how one edge changed

Each frame is the edge at one time. Red is what the frame added.

Dashed lines are observation, solid lines are experiment, dotted lines are conjecture. Width is certainty. The small figures are sources.

The strip reads left to right. The graph holds temperature and homicide at 1.07 per 5 °C. New York and Chicago give 1.15 and 1.13 for shootings, registered and refereed. Three steps were tested: people outdoors (311 street-noise complaints) removes 44% of the slope and is confirmed; intoxication removes 14% and warm nights give 1.03, both unsettled. The last frame is a conjecture, drawn dotted with a question mark: antipsychotic medicines raise the risk of death in a heat wave (Chen 2025, odds ratio 2.43, not in the graph), and no study was found of whether they change the effect of heat on violence.
