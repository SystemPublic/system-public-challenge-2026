Edges as objects: what is known about each link, and how links relate

The System Public Graph links variables by findings from papers. This page draws each link as an object with contents: every finding the graph holds for it, the study design where one is known, whether this project read the abstract, and the city's registered test where there is one. It then shows relations among the links themselves.

A. The edge field

Each row is one edge. The bar is a log-ratio axis from 0.25 to 4, with no effect at the centre line, and each mark is one finding. Findings that are not ratios are counted in the end boxes, negative at the left and positive at the right. The coloured capsule is the city's estimate and 95% interval. Hover a row for a summary, and click it to open the edge in part B.
