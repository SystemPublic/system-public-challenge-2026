Strips: how an edge changes

A graph of findings changes whenever a study, a dataset or a retraction arrives. Each strip below shows one such change as a sequence of frames. A frame is the state of a few edges at one time. What a frame adds is drawn in yellow, and what it withdraws is drawn in red. Every number has a numbered source under the strip.

The grammar of arrows

Five properties of an edge are drawn, and each has one visual channel. The line says what kind of evidence there is. The head says which way the influence runs. The width says how certain the edge is. The colour says what changed in this frame. A mark on the line says what is open or withdrawn.

Built from viz/data/comics.js. The grammar and the rules for writing a strip are in docs/comics.md.
