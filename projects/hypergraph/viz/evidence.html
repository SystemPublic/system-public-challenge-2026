A. Test cards

Each card is one claim, drawn on the scale of its own estimate. Ratios use a logarithmic axis, so that a halving and a doubling are the same distance from 1. The shaded band is the range the registrant predicted before seeing the data. Ticks marked S and R are the thresholds in the registration's support and refute conditions; where they coincide with no effect they sit on the dashed line. The filled mark and line are the city estimate and its 95% interval, coloured and shaped by the verdict, which is also printed. A hollow mark below it is a placebo: the same design applied where or when no effect can exist. A short vertical stroke above the estimate is a referee's re-estimate. B. What review changed

Each line is a claim whose current verdict differs from the verdict first scored, read from the outcome's history. The left column is the first verdict and the right column the current one. A move toward the middle goes from confirmed or contested to unsettled or no test. Claims whose verdict did not change are counted beside each column but not drawn.

C. Lineage of one claim

The timeline places each event at its recorded time. The vertical line is the registration time (registered_at, written into the hashed registration file); the registration commit is marked in the same lane. Data fetches to its right were made after the prediction was fixed. A fetch to its left is coloured by the class an audit gave it (docs/lineage_audit.md) and is listed with the evidence below the chart. Dataset update times are the publisher's rowsUpdatedAt; those far earlier than the registration are pinned to the left edge with their date. The chain below follows the claim from its registration hash through the dataset versions, the code and environment, to the number, the referee and the verdict.

D. Concordance strip by topic

This is the strip from the evidence dashboard with plain titles and grouped by topic. The axis runs from −1, where the city data contradict the direction the research reports, through 0, where they cannot tell, to +1, where they agree. The coloured bar is the interval on this scale and the mark its central value. The scale has no units; section A shows each estimate in its own units. Rows marked "no value" have no stance, because the city effect has no null that expresses the research direction (rule 6) or because the claim was not tested.
