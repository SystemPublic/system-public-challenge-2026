Neighbourhood

The concept chosen above sits at the centre. Each other circle is a concept that published findings link to it. In the radial layout, concepts with more findings sit closer to the centre. The width of a line shows the number of findings behind the link. The graph's own totals do not say whether the findings point up or down, so the colour comes from a sample of individual findings for the links with the most findings: blue where most sampled findings are positive, vermillion where most are negative, and grey where they are mixed or null. A light dashed grey line is a link whose findings were not sampled, so its sign is unknown. Select a line or a circle to list the findings, with links to PubMed.
