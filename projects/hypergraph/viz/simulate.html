This page composes associations, some measured in other cities and several assumed. It is not a causal model of New York. The link from vehicle entries to speed failed its held-out test, and day-to-day data do not fix its sign, so its range crosses zero. Speed, ambulance time and survivors can therefore move either way, and the last column gives the share of parameter draws in which each outcome improves. For those three outcomes that share is the probability that the link has the helpful sign, and it is the same for every schedule that lowers entries. A higher toll lowers entries in every draw by assumption, and a schedule scaled to hold revenue lowers entries by construction. Revenue is the only outcome on which schedules differ by more than their uncertainty. The count of survivors is an illustration of what the chain implies and is not a forecast. Baselines are 2025 means. The write-up is docs/policy_simulation.md in the repository.

Each value is read from data/derived/simulate/parameters.json. Type a is estimated in this project, b is a finding in the System graph read against its abstract, c is an assumption with a wide range. Each central value is the median of its draws. The sign column says whether every estimate has the sign shown (measured), whether the range crosses zero because the evidence does not fix the sign (open), or whether the sign is assumed.
