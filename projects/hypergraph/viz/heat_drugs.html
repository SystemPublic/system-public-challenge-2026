Ambient heat is at the centre. Each spoke leads to a drug class (left) or a chronic condition (right). Each mark on a spoke is one published finding, read in its PubMed abstract: its shape is the study design, its size the effect (the log of the ratio), its colour the direction. A solid spoke means the System Public Graph holds at least one record of a finding on that link (correct, or with its numbers right and a qualifier lost); a dashed spoke means the studies exist but the graph search did not find them; a dotted grey spoke means no study was found in either. Filled marks are in the graph; hollow marks are not. Click a label for the findings. Method and sources: docs/heat_drugs.md.
