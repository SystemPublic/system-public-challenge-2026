Neighbourhood explorer

Pick a neighbourhood on the map or from the list. The profile shows film shoots, vacant lots, new restaurants, coffee shops, galleries and arts workers, and how the place ranks among the city's residential neighbourhoods. All numbers are descriptive counts from open data; none is a tested claim.

Areas are the 262 Neighborhood Tabulation Areas (NTAs) that the Department of City Planning drew for the 2020 census; ranks use the residential NTAs with at least 5,000 residents. Sources: Mayor's Office of Media and Entertainment film permits (current table and two archived copies; July to December 2022 are missing), PLUTO 26v2 and 10v1, DOHMH restaurant inspections, Dining Out NYC licences, OpenStreetMap, and the American Community Survey 2008-2012 and 2018-2022. Film shoots are counted where a permit's street segment could be located (83% of segments). A "new" restaurant has a permit number issued since about September 2024 and at least one inspection. Methods and limits are in docs/explore.md; the scripts are in analyses/explore/.
