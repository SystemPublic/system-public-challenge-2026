What changed between two versions?

Pick two versions of a collection of tested claims on the Underlay. The page asks the Underlay for the difference, looks up each claim's relationship in System's public graph, and draws one strip per claim. Yellow was added, red was withdrawn. Nothing below is stored: every strip is drawn from the records when you press the button.
