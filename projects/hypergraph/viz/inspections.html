Our intuition is off. The leading cause is a sick worker. Then come temperature and handling. Pests come last.

How to read a score, a hearing and a closure

An inspector adds points for each violation, so a lower score is better. 0 to 13 points earns an A, 14 to 27 a B, and 28 or more a C. A restaurant can contest violations at a hearing. When a violation is dismissed, its points come off. "Closed by DOHMH" means the health department shut the kitchen at that inspection until it was fixed; those restaurants usually reopen. The restaurants counted as gone have left the city's table altogether.
