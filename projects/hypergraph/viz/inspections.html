How to read a score. An inspector adds points for each violation, so a lower score is better. 0 to 13 points earns an A, 14 to 27 a B, and 28 or more a C. A restaurant can contest violations at a hearing. When a violation is dismissed, its points come off, which is why a score can fall after the inspection and does not rise. "Closed by DOHMH" means the health department shut the kitchen at that inspection until it was fixed. Those restaurants usually reopen, so they are still in the table. The 519 "gone" restaurants are different: they have left the table altogether.
