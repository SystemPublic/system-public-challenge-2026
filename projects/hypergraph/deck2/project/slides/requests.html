Requests to System and to the Underlay

Two fields would let any collection draw its own strips

1. Store direction and design

Record which variable of a finding is the exposure, and the study design. The line style and the arrow head of a strip come from these. Design is recorded for 20 of 615 studies.

2. Record withdrawals

A placebo, a failed replication or a retraction should be a record that points at the finding it changes, at a fixed version. Then a mass replication result becomes one strip.

Already there: version differences

The Underlay returns what was added, changed and removed between two versions, and System’s public graph is on it at version 1.0.0. Our generator needs nothing else from it.

These come from the audit work. They are offered as the feedback a public beta is for.
