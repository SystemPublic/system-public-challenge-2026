A strip: our own conjecture set aside

Yesterday we proposed a link. Today’s evidence counts against it, and the strip records both.

Red with a cross is a withdrawal. The risk of death in heat for people on these medicines stays, and a city can act on that.

We had proposed that medicines which impair cooling might explain part of the rise in shootings with heat. No study exists, and public prescription data give the prescriber's ZIP code, which our check shows is a weak stand-in for where patients live. The evidence we could gather counts against the idea: psychiatric ambulance calls rise 1.021 per 5 °C against 1.146 for shootings and remove none of the slope; a Medicare study (Layton 2020) found no interaction of heat waves with these medicines; shootings inside do not rise. The conjecture was never a registered claim, so this is a line of work withdrawn, not a test failed. What stays is the health finding: Chen 2025, odds ratio of death 2.43 in a heat dome for people with schizophrenia who had an antipsychotic dispensed.
