Each prediction was hashed and committed before its data were read. A referee who had not seen the reasoning rebuilt the deciding number for 90 of the 96 outcomes. Referees changed eleven verdicts, eight toward unsettled, and thirteen reading rules came out of their criticism. Two claims in these counts were not blind tests.

97 claims are registered and one is still open. The three claims of 14 November (the full moon and the two inside-and-outside windows, which are one result) were refereed the same morning; the referee found our standard error too small by a factor of 1.7 and our reading of the mechanism too strong, and both were corrected. The two claims that were not blind are afford-gentrification-prevalence and heat-extremes-downturn. The published Underlay collection is version 1.0.0 with 94 claims.
