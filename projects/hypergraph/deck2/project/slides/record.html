Each prediction was hashed and committed before its data were read. A referee who had not seen the reasoning rebuilt the deciding number for 91 of the 97 outcomes. Referees changed eleven verdicts, eight toward unsettled, and thirteen reading rules came out of their criticism. Two claims in these counts were not blind tests.

98 claims are registered and one is still open. The four claims of 14 November (the full moon, the two inside-and-outside windows in New York, which are one result, and the Chicago replication) were refereed the same morning; the referees found our standard errors too small, our reading of the mechanism too strong and our Chicago location classes flawed, and all three are in the record.
