The idea · Team Hypergraph

An edge is a small, continuing meta-analysis. We add a city’s test to it.

69 edges, 1,123 findings from 625 papers, 25 edges with a city test. The links between variables are the objects of study here, more than the variables. Mark shape: square is an experiment, diamond a quasi-experiment, triangle a cohort or case-crossover, dot a cross-section, a thin tick means the graph records no design. The graph records a study design for 20 of 615 studies; our abstract reading adds 117 findings. Live page: viz/hypergraph.html.
