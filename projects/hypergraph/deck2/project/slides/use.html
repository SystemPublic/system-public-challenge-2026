What a city can use

Three results, each one a change to an earlier reading

Heat acts on shootings outdoors

Outside 1.17 per 5 °C, inside 1.01. Street activity carries 44% of the slope. Hot-evening work belongs on the street. A short search found no published estimate of this split for shootings.

Safety evaluations need a placebo year

Red-light cameras and turn calming both looked effective. The same comparison a year earlier, with nothing installed, gave nearly the same number.

Results need a fixed version

In five months the city removed 519 restaurants from its inspection table and lowered 485 scores. A result cited by date cannot be rerun. A versioned collection can.
