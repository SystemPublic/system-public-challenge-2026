A grammar of arrows: one visual channel for each property of an edge

A strip is the difference between versions of the graph. To draw one automatically, the graph needs three fields it lacks: which variable is the exposure, the study design, and a record of withdrawal.

Line style is the kind of evidence, the head is direction, width is certainty and not effect size, colour is what changed in the frame. Sources are numbered under each strip with a DOI, a PubMed identifier or a registered claim. Because the two Underlay collections are versioned, the difference between two versions gives the red, black and grey marks directly; only the caption needs a writer. The graph records study design for 20 of 615 studies and has no exposure field. docs/comics.md.
