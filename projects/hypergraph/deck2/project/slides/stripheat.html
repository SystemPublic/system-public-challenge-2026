A strip: one edge over time

Each frame is the edge at one time. Yellow is what the frame added.

Dashed is observation, solid is experiment, dotted is conjecture. Width is certainty. A diamond is a city’s registered test. The small figures are sources.

The graph holds temperature and homicide at 1.07 per 5 °C; it had stored the studies' percent changes as odds ratios, which we corrected from the abstracts. New York and Chicago give 1.15 and 1.13 for shootings, registered and refereed. Three steps were tested: people outdoors (311 street-noise complaints) removes 44% of the slope and is confirmed; intoxication removes 14% and warm nights give 1.03, both unsettled.
