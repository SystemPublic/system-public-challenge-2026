# Story patients: routing results (SYNTHETIC)

Every person here is synthetic (data/processed/synthetic). Generated by `scripts/routing_story.py` from
`contacts_routed.csv` and `cooling_match.csv`. Routing engine: public OSRM (FOSSGIS, routing.openstreetmap.de; OpenStreetMap network); see README.md in this folder for methods and caveats.

- Contact rule (spec §4.4a): travel <= T_max - 15 min (patient p90) - 15 min (contact p90). Travel allowance = 15 / 30 / 60 min for the 45 / 60 / 90 min budgets.
- Walking uses an older-adult pace (0.8 m/s) on the routed distance. Driving is free-flow (no traffic, no parking), so it is optimistic; "no car" = walking or subway only.
- Subway times are an approximation (straight line between nearest stations), not GTFS routing.
- Cooling places: reference heat day 2025-06-23 (Monday, peak heat index 105.7 F in the replay). Leave-by = arrive before 11 AM.
