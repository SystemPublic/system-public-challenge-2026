HOW TO READ THIS TOOL

Evidence, not a prediction engine.

All numeric effects below come from the team's paper-verified trial records. Different outcomes, comparators and effect measures are shown separately. A confidence interval that includes 1 does not establish a statistically significant difference for a ratio measure. These are illustrative planning options, not clinical guidance, a country ranking, or forecasts of cases averted.

Vaccination, SMC eligibility and net procurement must be checked against local epidemiology, current WHO/national guidance, delivery capacity and budget. The tool does not establish that a combination will have the sum of individual trial effects.
